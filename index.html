Four subjects of one model is a small basis. It settles the question anyway because the threshold is fifty times smaller.

Wang et al. ran 24 different reasonable prompts with model and items fixed. Frontier models cluster within 2–4% of each other — so on MMLU the measurement error is larger than the differences being measured (signal/noise 0.67; MMLU-Pro reaches 1.50).

This variance does not shrink with more items. Sampling error falls as one over root n; prompt sensitivity does not fall at all. A bigger benchmark cannot fix it.

The standard check fires on noise

A run loses 18 items and gains 15 — a coin flip, McNemar p = 0.73. The aggregate-delta rule fires. The paired test stays silent.
